About

Time Ball is a physics puzzle: draw a line, drop the ball, get to the goal as fast as you can.

Beat the gold time on a circuit and you'll race a ghost of your fastest run on every attempt after.

All records are stored locally on this device. Global leaderboards would require a server backend.
